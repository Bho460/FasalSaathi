⚠️ Zaroori baat

Yeh tool patterns aur possibilities dikhata hai, faisla nahi karta. Final decision se pehle apne KVK / krishi adhikari se baat karein, mitti ki jaanch (soil test) karwayein, aur local mandi bhav dekhein.

Data ki sachchai: Climate numbers NASA POWER se aate hain (satellite + model, ~50 km grid), kisi ek khet ka ground-measurement nahi. Soil moisture yahan model-based hai. Vikasnagar pilot point par MODIS NDVI (250 m) aur SMAP soil moisture (9 km) jude hain, aur MODIS ET (500 m, sirf 2021–2023 ke 3 saal) bhi; ye sab sirf isi point ke liye hain. MODIS ET sinchai ko aksar kam pakadta hai, isliye ise sirf sanket samjhein, paani ki zaroorat ka final aankda nahi; GRACE (groundwater) aur baaki jagahon ka data agla step hai. SMAP ka 9 km pixel kai khet milakar average deta hai. Practices aur bimariyon ki list ICAR ke Uttarakhand profile se hai (kuch aankde source mein typo jaise lagte hain, isliye number slides mein dene se pehle original source se confirm karein). Crop scores rule-based hain (expert-guided estimates), isliye har score ke saath ± range dikhti hai.

📖 Shabdkosh

Evapotranspiration (ET)Mitti aur paudhon se udne wala paani. ET zyada = fasal ko zyada sinchai chahiye.
NDVISatellite se paudhe ki "hariyali" ka score. Kam NDVI = khet khali ya fasal tanaav mein.
Soil moisture anomalyMitti ki nami normal se kitni kam/zyada hai.
Nitrogen fixingDalhan fasal (moong, chana, berseem) jadon se hawa ka nitrogen mitti mein jodti hain, yani urea kam.
Cover crop / hari khadKhet khali na rahe isliye lagayi fasal (sunhemp, dhaincha). Erosion rokti hai, organic matter badhati hai.
Satellite vs ground dataSatellite bade ilake ka pattern deta hai; aapke khet ki asli nami/mitti sirf zameen par naapne se pata chalti hai. Dono mila kar sabse achha.
