⚠️ Important

This tool shows patterns and possibilities; it does not make decisions. Before deciding, talk to your KVK / agriculture officer, get a soil test done, and check local market prices.

Data honesty: Climate numbers come from NASA POWER (satellite + model, ~50 km grid), not from ground measurements at a single field. Soil wetness there is model-based. At the Vikasnagar pilot point, MODIS NDVI (250 m), SMAP soil moisture (9 km) and MODIS ET (500 m, only 3 years, 2021–2023) are linked; these apply only to this point. MODIS ET often under-captures irrigation, so treat it as an indication, not a final water requirement. GRACE (groundwater) and data for other locations are next steps. A 9 km SMAP pixel averages many fields together. The practices and disease list come from the ICAR Uttarakhand profile (some figures in the source look like typos, so confirm with the original source before quoting numbers). Crop scores are rule-based (expert-guided estimates), which is why each score shows a ± range.

📖 Glossary

Evapotranspiration (ET)Water lost from soil and plants to the air. Higher ET = the crop needs more irrigation.
NDVIA satellite score of how green plants are. Low NDVI = bare field or a stressed crop.
Soil moisture anomalyHow much drier or wetter the soil is than normal.
Nitrogen fixingLegumes (moong, chickpea, berseem) add nitrogen from the air to the soil through their roots, so less urea is needed.
Cover crop / green manureA crop grown so the field is not left bare (sunhemp, dhaincha). It reduces erosion and adds organic matter.
Satellite vs ground dataSatellites show the pattern over a large area; the real moisture and soil of your field can only be measured on the ground. Using both together works best.
